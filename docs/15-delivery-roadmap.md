# 15 — Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Product validation

Duration: 1–2 weeks.

Deliverables:

- landing page,
- product positioning,
- pilot-user list,
- 20 user interviews,
- 50 manually evaluated products,
- validation report.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Duration: 2–3 weeks.

Deliverables:

- Laravel setup,
- authentication,
- organizations,
- roles,
- policies,
- Filament,
- global country and currency reference data,
- organization market preferences,
- plans,
- usage tracking,
- test foundation.

## Phase 2 — Buy Analysis MVP

Duration: 3–5 weeks.

Deliverables:

- listing intake,
- images,
- AI extraction,
- product matching,
- manual comparables,
- price estimate,
- cost calculator,
- risk score,
- deal score.

## Phase 3 — Sell Analysis MVP

Duration: 2–4 weeks.

Deliverables:

- owned-product intake,
- price bands,
- sale-speed selection,
- listing title,
- listing description,
- photo checklist,
- sale portfolio.

## Phase 4 — Outcome tracking

Duration: 2 weeks.

Deliverables:

- purchase records,
- sale records,
- actual costs,
- actual profit,
- estimate accuracy.

## Phase 5 — Alerts and subscriptions

Duration: 3–4 weeks.

Deliverables:

- Stripe,
- plans,
- email,
- Telegram,
- saved searches,
- matching pipeline.

## Phase 6 — Approved data connectors

Duration: source-dependent.

Deliverables:

- connector registry,
- CSV,
- email feeds,
- partner feeds,
- approved APIs,
- compliance documentation.

## Commercial target

A chargeable pilot version should be possible after Phases 1–4.

Expected range for two experienced developers:

```text
10–14 weeks
```

provided the initial scope remains narrow.
